## Prerequisites

Before starting, ensure you have:

- A valid email address for registration
- Access to Stripe or Razorpay for payments (optional for initial setup)
- Your custom domain ready (optional)

<Callout kind="info">

UltraLMS operates by invitation. Request one to get started immediately.

</Callout>

## Get Started in Minutes

Follow these steps to set up your account, access the dashboard, create your first AI-powered course, and publish your academy.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request an Invitation" icon="mail">

    Visit the [UltraLMS homepage](https://ultralms.com) and click **Request an invitation**.

    Fill out the form with your academy details. You'll receive an email within minutes containing your unique invitation link.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Sign In and Access Dashboard" icon="login">

    Click **I have an invitation** on the homepage or use your invitation link.

    Enter your credentials to reach the dashboard at `academy.yourdomain.com/admin`.

    Key sections include **Overview**, **Courses**, **People**, **Marketing**, **Automations**, **Sales**, **Website**, and **Live**.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Create Your First Course with AI" icon="zap">

    In the dashboard, select **New course with AI**.

    Provide a brief outline, such as:

    ```
    A six-week course on watercolor landscapes for complete beginners.
    Beginner level, 6 modules, warm practical tone.
    ```

    AI generates modules, lessons, quizzes, narration, slides, and a landing page. Review and revise as needed.

    <CodeGroup tabs="API,Webhook">

    ````javascript
    // Use REST API to create course programmatically
    const response = await fetch('https://api.ultralms.com/v1/courses', {
      method: 'POST',
      headers: {
        'Authorization': `Bearer ${YOUR_API_KEY}`,
        'Content-Type': 'application/json'
      },
      body: JSON.stringify({
        title: 'Watercolor Landscapes',
        aiPrompt: 'Six-week beginner course on watercolor',
        modules: 6
      })
    });
    ````

    ````bash
    # Webhook for course creation events
    curl -X POST https://your-webhook-url.com/ultralms \
      -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
      -d '{
        "event": "course.created",
        "data": {"id": "course-123", "title": "Watercolor Landscapes"}
      }'
    ````

    </CodeGroup>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Publish and Share Your Academy" icon="globe">

    Once your course is ready (e.g., `Draft` status with lessons and quizzes), click **Publish**.

    Customize your landing page, enable certificates (verifiable by QR code), and set up welcome emails/automations.

    Share via `academy.yourdomain.com` or embed the course player on your site.

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Custom Domain" icon="settings">

        Configure your domain in **Website > Custom domains**.

        Point DNS: Add CNAME `academy` to `ultralms.com`.

      </Tab>

      <Tab title="Embed Player" icon="code">

        Use the embed code:

      ````html
      <iframe
        src="https://academy.yourdomain.com/embed/course-123"
        width="100%"
        height="600"
        frameborder="0">
      </iframe>
      ````

      </Tab>
    </Tabs>

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="success">

Congratulations! Your academy is live. Students can now enroll, complete courses, and earn certificates.

</Callout>

- **Invitation not received?** Check spam folder or request again.
- **Dashboard inaccessible?** Verify your subdomain `academy.yourdomain.com`.
- **AI course generation fails?** Ensure prompt is under 500 characters.
