## Get Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Courses">
    Log in, select "Courses" from the UltraLMS dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Create Course'">
    Fill in the course title, description, and upload a cover image.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add lessons">
    Click "Add Lesson" to upload videos, texts, or quizzes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Review settings, then click "Publish". Your course goes live to students.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You're ready to teach on UltraLMS!
