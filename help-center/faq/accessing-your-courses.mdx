## Where are my courses?

Log in to your UltraLMS dashboard at `https://app.ultralms.com`.

- Click "My Learning" in the sidebar.
- All your current courses appear here.
- Select a course to continue learning.

**Tip:** If you don't see a course:

- Check that you're logged into the correct account.
- Courses are visible after enrollment/payment.
- For gifted courses, check your email for an invite link.

Contact support if your courses are missing.
