---
title: Videos Not Playing
description: Fix UltraLMS video playback problems.
---

## Symptoms

- Course video won't load or shows a blank area
- Error: "Video unavailable"

## Quick Fixes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your browser">
    Use a supported browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Disable browser extensions">
    Especially ad blockers or privacy extensions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear cache and reload">
    Press Ctrl+F5 or clear browser data, then refresh.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try another device or network">
    Some firewalls can block video content.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If issues continue, contact UltraLMS support.
